1. What PadMe is and what it works with

PadMe is an Android app that lets you browse and change the kits and samples stored on a drum sampling pad connected to your phone by USB, and make and restore backups of the pad's memory. It works on your phone only and needs no account or internet connection. How PadMe handles data is described in the privacy policy.

Please check this list before you buy PadMe. PadMe works with:

  • Pads: the Roland SPD-SX, switched to Wave Manager mode. Other models, including the SPD-SX PRO and the SPD-SX SE, and pads of other makers are not supported yet. If we add a model, the store listing will say so.
  • Phones: Android 8.0 or newer with USB OTG support, and a USB OTG cable or adapter.
  • Sounds you add: WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC and other audio formats your phone's Android can decode. PadMe writes them to the pad as 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV, the format the pad plays.
  • Backups: zip files made by PadMe of a whole pad.

3. Back up your pad

PadMe writes to your instrument's memory. A mistake, a bug, a cable that comes loose or a phone that runs out of battery during a write can leave kits or samples damaged or missing. Before you change a pad with PadMe:

  • make a full backup with "Back up the pad" and keep it somewhere other than the phone, for example on a computer or in cloud storage;
  • make another backup before a gig, and do not change the pad's memory shortly before you play;
  • switch the pad off before you unplug it.

PadMe keeps an undo history and offers a backup when it sees a pad for the first time, but these do not replace your own backup.
