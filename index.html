PadMe

Kit editor for sample pads. Change the kits and samples on your sampling pad from an Android phone connected by USB. No computer, no account, no internet needed.

PadMe is not on Google Play yet.

Works with

  • Pads: Roland SPD-SX in Wave Manager mode. Other models, including the SPD-SX PRO and SE, and pads of other makers are not supported yet.
  • Phones: Android 8.0 or newer with USB OTG, and a USB OTG cable or adapter.

What it does

  • Shows every kit with its pads, trigger inputs and footswitches.
  • Puts samples on pads, sets volume and pan, renames kits and copies them.
  • Adds sounds from your phone (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC and more) in the format the pad plays.
  • Trims, renames and previews samples, and makes a copy of a phrase at a new tempo.
  • Undoes recent changes and puts back a change a loose cable cut short.
  • Backs up the whole pad to one zip file and restores it.
  • In English, Polish, Ukrainian and German.

Keep your own backup of the pad before you change it: PadMe writes to your instrument's memory.

Privacy

PadMe sends nothing over the internet except, if you agree, a report when it crashes, without names or anything about your pad. Your samples, kits and history stay on your phone and your pad. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
